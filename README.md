## Meet Marvi

Marvi is there when you need her and quiet when you don't. Call her by voice, speak naturally, and interrupt at any time. Her Dynamic Island stays tucked at the edge of your screen, then opens briefly for a conversation, an update, or a decision that needs you.

When you want a closer look, the control center brings your conversations, memories, connected services, room, and preferences together in one place.

## One assistant for more of your life

**Talk and keep moving.** Ask a question while you work, switch to typing when you need more room, or let Marvi read a response aloud. She can take on longer tasks and tell you when the work is done.

**Remember what matters.** Marvi Cortex keeps useful preferences, people, and moments close at hand. Explore what she remembers, trace a memory to its source, correct it, or erase it.

**Stay connected.** Bring in the accounts and tools you already use. Marvi can help with files, the web, schedules, and everyday desktop tasks from one familiar place. Telegram lets you reach the same assistant when you're away from your computer.

**Feel at home.** With Smart Room connected, Marvi can respond to presence, room conditions, devices, and meaningful events. Choose how she sounds and make the Island and control center feel like yours.

## Present, with you in control

Your microphone and camera streams stay on your machine. Choose local models or connect a provider you trust. Decide what Marvi can access, what she remembers, and when she may speak up.

In **Confirm** mode, Marvi asks before actions that need your approval. In **YOLO** mode, you can give her more freedom, with a clear on-screen indicator and an activity history you can review.
