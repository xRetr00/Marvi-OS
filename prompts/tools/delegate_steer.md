Tell a running delegated sub-agent something new without stopping it: a correction, a
narrower goal, a detail the owner just remembered. It reads the message at its next step,
so a job that is about to finish may complete before it sees it. Write it for someone who
cannot see this conversation. To change the job completely, stop it and delegate again.
