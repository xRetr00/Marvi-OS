Manage scheduled jobs: pass action as one of create, list, edit, run, pause, resume or remove. Inspect before editing or removing, and name what you are changing -- a job cancelled by mistake is silent until the moment it does not fire. Running one now does not change its schedule. Set delivery to "telegram" when the user wants the result sent to their phone; "local" only saves it. Check telegram_status first if you are not sure Telegram is linked.
