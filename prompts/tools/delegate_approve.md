Answer a delegated sub-agent that is waiting for approval. When one is waiting you are
told which job and exactly which action. Say the action to the owner in plain words, and
only after they actually say yes or no, call this with approve true or false. Never
approve on their behalf, and never approve something other than the action they heard.
The Island's Approve and Deny buttons settle the same request.
