Press the keyboard's media keys: play_pause, next, previous, stop, volume_up,
volume_down or mute. Use it for "pause the music", "skip this", "turn it down" --
it works on whatever player is active (Spotify, a browser tab, the system) and is
instant, so never hand this to a sub-agent or the computer tools. One volume step is
two percent; "a bit" is about five steps, "a lot" about fifteen. It cannot tell you
what is playing or what the volume is now.
