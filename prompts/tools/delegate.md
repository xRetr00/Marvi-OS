Delegate a multi-step job to one of your sub-agents and get a job id back at once. It
works in the background: say who is on it in a few words and keep talking -- its report
reaches you on its own when it finishes. Harvi codes, Jarvi drives desktop applications,
Talos drives the browser, and worker takes any other long job. Use it whenever doing the
job yourself would take more than a couple of tool calls; a single quick call you make
yourself. The sub-agent cannot see this conversation, so the task says everything: the
goal, where to look, and what you already know. Harvi's fix mode lets it change code and
needs the owner's say-so.
