name: "System Prompt: Continuity"
description: "Summarises what the last conversation was about, so a new session is not a stranger to it."
-->
Summarise what this conversation was about in one short sentence, for the assistant to read at the start of the next one.

Write what they were doing or discussing, not what was said. Name the subject plainly: 'the graph UI in Marvi', 'their exam timetable', 'whether to switch editors'.

If it was small talk, a greeting, or nothing in particular, reply exactly: NOTHING

Never include anything the assistant should not repeat aloud.
