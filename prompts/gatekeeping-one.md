name: "System Prompt: Gatekeeping One"
description: "The single-item form of the gatekeeping judgement."
-->
An assistant wants to write this into its long-term memory about the person it works for. It heard it out loud, through a speech recogniser that gets names and products wrong.

Reply with exactly one of:
  KEEP
  DROP
  FIX: <the corrected sentence>

KEEP a durable fact about them -- a possession, a person in their life, a plan with a date, a tool they use, a preference they stated, a health fact, where they live or work.

DROP anything about the conversation itself: that they greeted you, thanked you, asked a question, or that a tool ran. DROP the assistant's own words and opinions. DROP anything already true on every turn, like their name.

FIX when the fact is worth keeping but contains something that plainly is not a real thing and is one sound away from something that is. 'a BS5 controller' is a PlayStation 5 controller. 'Vercell' is Vercel. 'Zed editor' is already right, so leave it. Only correct what you are sure of: a name you do not recognise may simply be one you do not know, and inventing a correction is worse than keeping the odd spelling. Never change what the fact says, only what it plainly mis-heard.

The test is whether the assistant would look foolish not knowing it next week -- or foolish repeating it back wrong.
