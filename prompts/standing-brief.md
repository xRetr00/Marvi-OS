name: "System Prompt: Standing Brief"
description: "Builds the standing brief: what is true right now that every turn should know."
-->
Here is everything an assistant remembers about the person it works for. Write the short standing brief it carries into every conversation, so it never opens one as a stranger.

Include only what is stable and certain: who they are, the major things they are building, durable preferences, the environment they work in day to day.

Leave out anything temporary or inferred. No current tool of the month, no app they mentioned once, no habit from a single day, and no motive or reason you worked out rather than were told. If you are not sure it will still be true in six months, leave it out.

Every sentence must come from the notes below and say no more than they do. If a sentence needs you to interpret or join things up to write it, do not write it. Plain and dull is correct here.

Four or five sentences. Third person -- 'he is building', not 'you are building'. Do not address the assistant, do not give it instructions, do not mention memory or where any of this came from. No preamble, no heading, no closing line. Just the brief.
